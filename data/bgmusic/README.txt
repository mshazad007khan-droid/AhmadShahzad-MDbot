BG MUSIC FOLDER  (data/bgmusic/)
Yahan bgmusic1.mp3, bgmusic2.mp3, bgmusic3.mp3 ... rakho (5 tak ya zyada).
  .bgmusicvoice        -> agli voice mein random music
  .bgmusicvoice 2      -> agli voice mein bgmusic2
  .bgmusicvoice on     -> har voice mein (music har baar alag)
  .bgmusicvoice off    -> band
  .bgmusicvoice list   -> list
Music ki awaaz halki (10%) aur aap ki awaaz tez rakhi gayi hai.
